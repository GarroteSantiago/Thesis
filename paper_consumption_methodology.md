# Papers consumption methodology

## notes.md structure

```Text
# Paper: {name}

## 1. Context
What problem existed before this paper?

## 2. Claim
What does this paper argue?

## 3. Evidence (Phase 2 — The Vision / The Model)
How do they prove it?

## 4. Limitations (Phase 3 — Critique)
What did they miss or assume?

## 5. Thesis Connection (Phase 4–5 — Hardware)
How does my actor hardware extend or answer this paper?

## 6. Open Questions
What do I still need to answer after reading this?
```

## Reading Phases

### Pass 1 — Skim (10 min)
- Get the shape of the paper.
- Answer:
  - What problem does it solve?
  - What is the main claim?
  - Does it connect to your thesis?
- Read only:
  - title
  - abstract
  - intro
  - section headers
  - conclusion

### Pass 2 — Understand (1 hour)

- Read fully but skip proofs/math
- Answer:
  - How do they solve the problem?
  - What evidence do they give?
  - What does this mean for your thesis?
- Write 3–5 bullet points in your notes.md

### Pass 3 — Critique (2–3 hours)

- Only for papers which I:
  - directly challenge
  - extend
  - build on
- Answer:
  - What assumptions do they make?
  - What did they get wrong or leave open?
  - How does your thesis address those gaps?
