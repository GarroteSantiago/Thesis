### Key papers
From the protocol change `research/sls/1-protocol/changes/2026-10-12-key-papers.md` (accepted).

Papers my argument depends on (key papers) are read in full by me. A paper is key if it passes at least one test:
1. **Sole or main evidence:** it is the only paper, or one of very few, answering an SQ.
2. **Load-bearing claim:** the problem or the gap rests on a claim it makes.
3. **Strongest counter-evidence:** it argues most strongly against the observation.
4. **Seed:** it is in `research/0-start/seeds.md`.

Key papers are proposed, with the test each passes, at 3c (provisional); updated at each 4b from the papers each accepted concept rests on; extended at 5c with any paper `research/sls/5-gap/gap.md` rests on; and read in full before the gap gate (5c) is decided. A 3c decision does not wait for my full reading.
