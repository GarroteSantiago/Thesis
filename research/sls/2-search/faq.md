# FAQ

**How do I pick the start set?**
- Papers that are clearly relevant and come from different communities, publishers, years, and authors, so the snowball does not stay inside one group [1].
- Wohlin suggests Google Scholar to find them, to avoid bias toward one publisher [1].
- Candidates, all to be checked against the protocol: Hewitt [2], Agha [3], Kay [4], Dally [5], Woodruff [6], Backus [7].

**How do I decide whether a candidate is in?**
- Look at title first, then abstract, then the full text if still unsure [1].
- Apply only the criteria in `1-protocol/step.md` §1.3. If you want a new criterion, log it in §1.6 first.

**When do I stop?**
- When an iteration finds no new papers [1]. Not when you are tired, and not when the matrix "looks done".

**Why not search databases with keywords instead?**
- Terms in this area are not settled (actor, object, message-driven, capability), so keyword searches miss papers. Snowballing follows the citations the authors themselves made [1].
