# SQ8

## Question
What empirical evidence shows that the hardware's execution model, rather than the language, libraries, tools or training, determines how programmers design and write programs, and how correct those programs are?

## Why it matters to the observation
The notes' first premise ("the machine model shapes how programs are written") rests only on two Turing Award lectures, which are essays, not measurements. If the evidence says the language or the runtime decides how people write programs, then changing the hardware does not change the programs on top of it, and the motivation needs another basis.
