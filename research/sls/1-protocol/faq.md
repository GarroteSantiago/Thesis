# FAQ

**Why write a protocol before searching?**
- So the search and selection do not bend to what you hope to find. That bias is what makes a review unsystematic [1].

**What makes a good review question?**
- It asks what the literature says, not what you will build.
- Example SQ: "Which systems provide message or actor abstractions at the operating system or hardware level?"

**Where do the selection criteria come from?**
- From the review questions. A paper is in if it helps answer one of them.
- Typical exclusions: not peer reviewed, not in a language you read, only mentions the topic in passing.

**What is a quality checklist for?**
- To weigh papers, not to judge authors. A paper that claims performance without measurement still gets in, but its claims count less [1].

**Who reviews the protocol?**
- Kitchenham and Charters recommend an outside review [1]. Your supervisor is the natural choice.
