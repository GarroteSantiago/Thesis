# SQ10

## Question
What does the literature report about the equivalence (duality) of message-passing and shared-memory system structures, and can either one be built on top of the other while keeping its properties (encapsulation, freedom from data races, interposition) and its performance?

## Why it matters to the observation
Lauer and Needham (1978/1979, *On the Duality of Operating System Structures*) argue that message-oriented and procedure-oriented systems are duals: a problem solved in one has a dual solution in the other. If the two are equivalent, the observation's gap may be a matter of which layer exposes which model, and putting messages in hardware may add nothing that a layer above cannot give.
