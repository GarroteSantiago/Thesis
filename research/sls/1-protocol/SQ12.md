# SQ12

## Question
Where do measured costs and scaling limits come from in actor and message-passing runtimes on conventional hardware, and do their authors identify the hardware's shared-memory model as the limiting factor, or report software techniques that remove it?

## Why it matters to the observation
The notes say compiler techniques made language-specific processors unnecessary for Smalltalk and Self. If current actor runtimes (measured, for example, with the Savina benchmark suite, Imam and Sarkar 2014, AGERE!) reach their limits for reasons that a message-based machine would not change, the design problem loses its "improve execution" half.
