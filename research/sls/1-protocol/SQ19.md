# SQ19

## Question
Since the end of Dennard scaling, which kinds of specialised architectures have been adopted and which have failed, and what factors (software compatibility, ecosystem, programmability, economics, kind of workload) do analyses give for the difference?

## Why it matters to the observation
H1 says the end of free single-processor speedups reopens room for specialised machines. The specialisation that succeeded may be narrow and data-parallel (for example the tensor processing unit, Jouppi et al., 2018, CACM), far from general-purpose actor machines, and the barriers that sank earlier machines (software compatibility, ecosystem) may not have changed. SQ6 looks at the past decline; this question checks whether the present actually differs.

## Origin
counter-evidence (added by skeptic)

## Kind
kinds, because the question itself asks which kinds of specialised architecture were adopted or failed, and the factors analyses give; a census of every specialised chip is outside its wording and would not change the answer H1 needs. Census is not offered for that reason; it stays my decision at gate 1c if I want it. (`research/sls/1-protocol/step.md`, *How much each question needs*)

## Unit
A kind of specialised architecture with its outcome (adopted or failed), or a factor an analysis gives for the difference (software compatibility, ecosystem, programmability, economics, kind of workload).

## Enough when
At least 10 papers serving it are read, in the reading order, then 3 in a row add nothing new.

## Reading order
Analyses that state factors first, adopted and failed kinds alternating: the Turing lecture on domain-specific architectures (P6), the tensor processing unit (P31), domain-specific hardware for machine learning (P146), the Berkeley view (P111), dark silicon (P24), research problems of domain-specific architectures (P145). Round 0 holds these 6 serving papers, all on the adopted side or on general limits; none analyses a specialised architecture that failed, so later rounds must look for that side before the stop rule can be applied.
