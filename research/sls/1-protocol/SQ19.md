# SQ19

## Question
Since the end of Dennard scaling, which kinds of specialised architectures have been adopted and which have failed, and what factors (software compatibility, ecosystem, programmability, economics, kind of workload) do analyses give for the difference?

## Why it matters to the observation
H1 says the end of free single-processor speedups reopens room for specialised machines. The specialisation that succeeded may be narrow and data-parallel (for example the tensor processing unit, Jouppi et al., 2018, CACM), far from general-purpose actor machines, and the barriers that sank earlier machines (software compatibility, ecosystem) may not have changed. SQ6 looks at the past decline; this question checks whether the present actually differs.
