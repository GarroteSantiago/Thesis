# SQ11

## Question
Why do developers of actor or message-passing programs also use shared state, locks or other concurrency mechanisms alongside messages, and what do they report that the pure message model cannot express or costs too much?

## Why it matters to the observation
The observation wants a system with no escape routes from its own rules. Tasharofi, Dinges and Johnson (2013, *Why Do Scala Developers Mix the Actor Model with other Concurrency Models?*, ECOOP) report that most of the large actor programs they studied mix actors with other concurrency models, partly because of shortcomings of the actor model itself. If programmers keep building escape routes where they have the choice, a machine that removes them may be unusable rather than cleaner.
