# SQ6

## Question
According to their designers and to later evaluations, why were language-directed, object-based and message-based machines abandoned, not adopted, or made unnecessary?

## Why it matters to the observation
The notes list earlier attempts (Intel 432, SOAR, J-Machine, Lisp machines) and hypothesise that the context that defeated them has changed (H1). The stated reasons for their decline are what that hypothesis must be checked against.
