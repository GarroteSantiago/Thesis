# Protocol change: i4-premise

Saved as `sls/1-protocol/changes/2026-10-16-i4-premise.md`.

## What changed
- `sls/1-protocol/criteria.md`, I4:
  - Before: "A paper is included if it reports evidence or an argument that directly answers one of SQ8–SQ19, whether or not it mentions messages or objects. (SQ8–SQ19)"
  - After: "A paper is included if it reports evidence or an argument about the premise that one of SQ8–SQ19 names in its *Why it matters* section (the claim of step 0 that the question attacks), whether or not it mentions messages or objects. A paper that addresses only the question's general topic does not meet I4. The verdict names the SQ and the premise. (SQ8–SQ19)"

## Why
Most papers entered through slack rules, and that is a problem.

## Papers affected
The 103 papers included in round 0 under I4 are re-screened against the new I4, in the same pass as `2026-10-16-quick-check.md`. Among the papers already read, only P22 entered through I4; it is re-checked from its record and full text. A paper that fails the new I4 and meets no other criterion is excluded, citing this change.
