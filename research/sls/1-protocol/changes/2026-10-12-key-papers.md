# Protocol change: key-papers

Saved as `sls/1-protocol/changes/2026-10-12-key-papers.md`.

## What changed
- `sls/3-reading/step.md`, *Rules*:
  - Before: "Papers my argument depends on are read in full by me." No test said which papers those are, and the 3c skill asked for them to be read before each 3c decision.
  - After: a paper is key if it passes at least one of four tests (sole or main evidence for an SQ; a claim the problem or gap rests on; strongest counter-evidence; a seed in `0-start/seeds.md`). Key papers are proposed at 3c (provisional), updated at each 4b from the papers each accepted concept rests on, extended at 5c with any paper `5-gap/gap.md` rests on, and read in full by me before the gap gate (5c) is decided.
- Outside the protocol, to match: `ai/flow-sls.md` rows 3c, 4b and 5c; the `sls-3-reading`, `sls-4-synthesis` and `sls-5-gap` skills.

## Why
Which papers I read in full was decided case by case, from the assistant's proposal, with no stated rule. At 3c the argument does not exist yet: it is built at synthesis (4a) and the gap (5a), so which papers it rests on can only be known from the concept files and `gap.md`.

## Papers affected
None re-screened or re-read. P8, P9, P10 and P12 no longer wait at 3c for my full reading; they are read before 5c if they are key.
