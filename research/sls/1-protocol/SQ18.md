# SQ18

## Question
What costs of object-based and capability-based hardware do independent evaluations attribute to object orientation, fine-grained protection or object addressing themselves, rather than to compilers or implementation, and how large are those costs?

## Why it matters to the observation
H2 (the 432's losses were avoidable) rests on one paper by the 432's own evaluators, and the same paper still names a residual 1–4 times slowdown as the inherent cost of the 432's style [notes, citing Colwell et al. 1988, pp. 336–337]. An earlier independent evaluation (Hansen et al., 1982, *A Performance Evaluation of the Intel iAPX 432*) and modern capability hardware (Woodruff et al., 2014, *The CHERI Capability Model*, ISCA) measure the cost from other angles. H2 holds only if the inherent part is small across sources, not in one.
