# FAQ

**What are the three passes?** [1]
1. **First pass, about 5–10 minutes:** title, abstract, introduction, section headings, conclusions, references. Enough to answer the five Cs.
2. **Second pass, up to an hour:** read with care, look at figures, mark references to follow. Skip proofs.
3. **Third pass, hours:** re-create the work in your head as if you were the author. Only for papers central to your design.

**What are the five Cs?** [1]
- **Category:** what kind of paper (measurement, analysis of an existing system, a prototype…).
- **Context:** which papers it relates to and what theory it uses.
- **Correctness:** whether its assumptions look valid.
- **Contributions:** what it adds.
- **Clarity:** whether it is well written.

**Why record the page for everything?**
- So every claim in the matrix and the thesis can be checked against its source [2].

**Do I need to read every paper three times?**
- No. Most papers stop at pass 1 or 2. Write down how deep you went.
