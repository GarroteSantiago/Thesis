## Reference

## First pass: the five Cs
- **Category:** Evaluation of a built system: a reduced instruction set computer for Smalltalk (SOAR), used to "estimate the performance implications of the Smalltalk-80 programming environment" (P13, abstract). Probably also a retrospective, as R0-015 proposes, but the abstract does not say so.
- **Context:** Exploratory programming environments such as Smalltalk-80 "require such expensive computers that few programmers can afford them"; SOAR ("Smalltalk on a RISC") was built by a research project at the University of California to widen that community (P13, abstract). Related work cited by the paper: not reachable.
- **Correctness:** Not assessable from the abstract. The abstract claims the authors "are now able to estimate" performance implications, which implies measurement on SOAR, but no figures, baselines or setup are given (P13, abstract).
- **Contributions:** (1) a list of "the demands that Smalltalk-80 places on traditional computer systems"; (2) "software and hardware ideas that answer those demands"; (3) an estimate of the performance implications of Smalltalk-80 (P13, abstract).
- **Clarity:** The abstract is clear about the aim and the structure of the article; the body was not read.
