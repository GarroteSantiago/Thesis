# FAQ

**How do I read the gap off the matrix?**
- Look for empty columns, and for concepts that never appear together in one row [1].
- A gap is only useful if it matters for the need stated in `1-protocol/need.md`.

**Which systems will show up as current solutions?**
- The included papers that already attempt what the observation says is missing, fully or in part.
- A list you knew before the review (`../0-start/seeds.md`) is **not verified**. Every claim needs its source and page.

**What history must my thesis answer?**
- If the idea was tried before and abandoned, why. Separate what failed because of the idea from what failed because of implementation choices: the second is often the opening.

**What counts as a limitation of the review?**
- Anything that could have kept relevant papers out: the start set, the criteria, papers you could not access, a single reviewer [2].
