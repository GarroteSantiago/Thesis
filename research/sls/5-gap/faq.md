# FAQ

**How do I read the gap off the matrix?**
- Look for empty columns, and for concepts that never appear together in one row [1].
- A gap is only useful if it matters for the need stated in `1-protocol/step.md` §1.1.

**Which systems will likely show up as current solutions?**
- Intel 432 [3], Transputer [4], J-Machine [5], CHERI (Capability Hardware Enhanced Reduced Instruction Set Computer Instructions) [6].
- An earlier draft matrix of these was **not verified**. Every claim needs its source and page.

**What history must my thesis answer?**
- Language-oriented hardware failed before [7].
- The Intel 432 was object-based hardware and was slow [8].
- Much of its slowness came from implementation choices, not only from the idea itself [8]. This is your opening.

**What counts as a limitation of the review?**
- Anything that could have kept relevant papers out: the start set, the criteria, papers you could not access, a single reviewer [2].
