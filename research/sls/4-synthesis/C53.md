# C53: Exploratory programming environments and their cost

## Definition
Environments such as Smalltalk-80 and the Lisp Machine, built for programmer productivity, which needed expensive machines. Merges "exploratory programming environment" (two records), "demands of Smalltalk-80 on traditional computer systems" and "price of Smalltalk".

## First found in
P11

## What the papers say, compared
- **Smalltalk.** Exploratory environments "remain largely experimental" because "They suffer from poor cost-performance"; the Dorado cost $120,000 (P11, pp. 6, 10–11). Ungar and Patterson's abstract lists Smalltalk-80's demands on traditional systems and says SOAR lets them estimate its performance cost (P13, abstract; abstract only).
- **Lisp.** The Lisp machine was meant to "support a Lisp development environment" (P15, p. 12); it is "an interactive system" with "no strong distinction between the 'system' programs and the 'user' programs" (P73, p. 282). Customers would not buy "expensive 'dedicated' computers" (P15, p. 35).
- **Comparison.** Both lines tried special hardware to make such environments affordable; both were overtaken by stock hardware (C40). The full text of P13 would show whether SOAR's authors agree.
