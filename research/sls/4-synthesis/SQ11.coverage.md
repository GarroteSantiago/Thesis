## Rule
- **Kind:** evidence.
- **Unit:** a finding: a reason developers give or a study reports for using shared state, locks or other mechanisms alongside messages, or a thing the pure message model is reported unable to express or too costly for.
- **Enough when:** at least 10 papers serving it are read, in the reading order, then 3 in a row add nothing new (10 + 3).
