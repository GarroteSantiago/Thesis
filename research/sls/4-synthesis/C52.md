# C52: Power as a many-core limit

## Definition
Power density and power that does not scale with voltage as limits on adding cores. Merges "voltage and frequency islands (DVFS)".

## First found in
P21

## What the papers say, compared
- **The SCC.** Its designers cite "the exponential surge in power density" (P21, p. 173) and split the chip into 8 voltage and 28 frequency islands (P21, pp. 173, 177–178). At low power the memory controllers become "the major power consumer, largely because the analog I/O voltages cannot be scaled due to the DDR3 spec" (P21, p. 180).
- **The J-Machine** reports 1.5 W per node without naming it as a limit (P65, p. 11).
- **Coherence.** The energy overhead of coherence is argued small but not modelled (P22, pp. 3–9).
- **Comparison.** Only one paper read measures power; the general limits (dark silicon, P24; the Berkeley view, P111) are unread.
