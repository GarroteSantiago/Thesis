# C24: Confinement by construction

## Definition
A trusted builder creates new processes and can tell, from their initial authority alone, whether they can communicate with third parties. Merges "factory", "constructor" and "clans and chiefs".

## First found in
P48

## What the papers say, compared
- **Static: from capabilities.** A KeyKOS factory "constructs other domains", and clients can determine whether its products are secure (P53, p. 7). An EROS constructor certifies whether a new process "has any ability to communicate with third parties" from its initial capabilities (P77, p. 180).
- **Dynamic: by redirection, abandoned.** L4's clans and chiefs redirected every message crossing a clan boundary to the clan's chief to support confinement; Liedtke claimed only two extra cycles per IPC for direct communication, but each redirection "adds two messages to a (logically) single round-trip IPC, a significant overhead", and it was "Abandoned" (P48, p. 139).
- **Comparison.** Confinement checked once at construction survived in the capability kernels; confinement enforced on every message did not survive in L4.
