# FAQ

**Why concepts and not authors?**
- An author-by-author review ("A says…, B says…") lists papers but does not compare them. Organizing by concept shows where papers agree, disagree, and stay silent [1].

**Where do the columns come from?**
- From the papers, as you read them. Add a column whenever a paper raises a concept the matrix does not have yet [1].
- Not from your objectives: the review describes the field as it is, before your design shapes it.

**What goes in the per-concept synthesis?**
- The thesis's literature chapter, in draft. One subsection per concept [1].
