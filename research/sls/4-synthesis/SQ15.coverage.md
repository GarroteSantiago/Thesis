## Rule
- **Kind:** evidence.
- **Unit:** a finding: a proof, measurement or argument that a system function needs global agreement or works better with central or logically central coordination, or what a decentralised version of it costs or gives up.
- **Enough when:** at least 10 papers serving it are read, in the reading order, then 5 in a row add nothing new (10 + 5).
