# Papers

PDFs of the papers read in the SLS, one per included paper, named `P{n}.pdf` after its P ID.

- **Where they come from:** I drop the ones I get myself here (e.g. through the university library). `reader` saves here every PDF it reaches on its own.
- **Where they are published:** as assets of the `papers` release of the thesis GitHub repository. The PDFs are not in git.
- **`just papers-push`** uploads every PDF here to that release. **`just papers-pull`** downloads them, e.g. on another machine.
- **`reader`** and **`citation-checker`** look here first (`ai/flow-sls.md`, row 3a).
