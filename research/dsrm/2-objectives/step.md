# Activity 2 — Define the objectives for a solution

> **Inputs:** accepted `1-problem/RQ*.md`, `sls/5-gap/solutions.md` · **Next:** `3-design/`

> Peffers [1]: infer the objectives from the problem definition and from knowledge of what is possible and feasible.
> - **Quantitative:** how a desirable solution would be better than current ones.
> - **Qualitative:** how the artifact is expected to support solutions to problems not addressed before.

## Rules
- Rigor aid (mine, not Peffers'): every objective says how it will be checked, so step 5 has something to compare against.
- Objectives, metrics and baselines are frozen before any result is seen.

## Produces
Templates in `ai/template/dsrm/2-objectives/`. Flow rows 7a–7c (`ai/flow-dsrm.md`).

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `O{n}.md` | One objective: what it traces to, kind, how it is checked, validation type |
| `metrics.md` | What is measured and how |
| `baselines.md` | What the artifact is compared with, and why that is fair |
