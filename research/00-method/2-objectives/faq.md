# FAQ

**Can an objective be binary (yes or no)?**
- Yes. Hevner accepts analytical and descriptive evaluation, not only numbers [1].
- Feasibility → binary. Evaluation → numeric threshold against a baseline.

**How do I write one?**
- Use three parts: *Property* + *Test* + *Pass condition*.
- Every objective traces to exactly one question.
- Two people running the test must agree on the result (*testability*).

**Vague vs checkable — what is the difference?**
- Vague: "Actors are isolated."
- Checkable: "No instruction lets an actor read another actor's memory."
- If people could argue about whether it passed, it is not precise enough yet.
