# FAQ

**I only read papers. When do I stop?**
- Reading must fill the matrix, not go on forever.
- Snowballing: follow references backward and citations forward. Stop when new papers change no cell [1].
- Three-pass reading: skim, then the main ideas, then a deep read only for central papers [2].
- Philosophy: *systematic over ad hoc*.

**How do I build the matrix?**
- Columns come from **your objectives**, not from the papers. This makes the review serve your thesis.
- Rows are systems that try to solve part of your design problem.
- Each cell is ✓, ~, or ✗, with its source and page.
- Philosophy: *requirements-driven review*.

**What is the research gap?**
- The combination of columns no row fully satisfies. It justifies why your thesis exists.

**Which systems are candidate rows?**
- Intel 432 [3], Transputer [4], J-Machine [5], CHERI (Capability Hardware Enhanced Reduced Instruction Set Computer Instructions) [6].
- The earlier draft matrix was **not verified**. Check every cell against its source.

**Which core papers do I start snowballing from?**
- Hewitt [7], Agha [8], Kay [9], Dally [5], Woodruff [6], Backus [10].
